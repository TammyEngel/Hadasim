The Wall Itself

The most important physical artifact connected with the Great Wall is the wall itself. Different sections were built during different historical periods. Some early sections were made from packed earth, while later sections used stone and bricks.

Objects Found Near the Wall

Archaeologists have found many objects connected with the soldiers and workers who lived near the Great Wall. These include pottery, tools, weapons and arrowheads. These objects help historians understand how people lived, worked and protected the border.

Some bricks also contain marks and inscriptions. These can give historians information about how the bricks were made and where different parts of the wall were constructed.
