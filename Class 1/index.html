The Great Wall of China

The Great Wall of China is one of the most famous historical structures in the world. It was built over many centuries by different Chinese states and dynasties. The wall was mainly created to protect northern borders from attacks and invasions. Over time, different parts of the wall were connected, rebuilt and strengthened. Today, the Great Wall is an important symbol of Chinese history and culture. This website explains how the wall began, who was involved in building it, what remains from that period, and why the wall is still important today.
